# Don't Be Awake with the Fisches

An original stylized 2D survival adventure for Nintendo 3DS. Explore a small boat, manage supplies, and discover what happens when you sleep on the open sea.

## Current rebuild

- **Main menu and difficulty:** Easy, Normal and Hard.
- **Captain selection:** choose a name and one of three outfit styles.
- **Four connected boat areas:** outer deck, kitchen, upper-deck garden and bedroom. Switch rooms with left/right or the lower-screen room buttons.
- **Fishing:** catch fish on the deck; bait and energy are limited.
- **Kitchen:** eat fish, harvested crops or stored meals to restore hunger.
- **Rainwater and gardening:** collect rainwater, plant seeds, water plants and harvest crops on the upper deck.
- **Sleep and day progression:** sleep in the bedroom to advance the day. Each night can be quiet, unsettling or bring a helpful surprise.
- **Survival systems:** hunger, thirst, energy and health.
- **Dual-screen presentation:** the upper screen shows a different illustrated scene for each room; the lower touchscreen contains supplies, status and controls.

## Controls

- **Left / Right:** move between rooms.
- **A:** interact with the current room.
- **B:** drink stored water.
- **X:** sleep when in the bedroom.
- **Touchscreen:** use the lower-screen room and action buttons.

## Build and testing

GitHub Actions builds the 3DSX and installable CIA with devkitPro. A successful workflow confirms compilation and packaging only; the CIA still needs to be tested on a real Nintendo 3DS. The current rebuild is an early gameplay foundation. Save/load, more detailed animations, sound, expanded events and additional story content remain future work.
